## About the Fellowship

Gayiti is a competitive AI engineering fellowship 
run by Gaya, a US-based AI automation startup that 
builds intelligent workflows and agents for insurance 
agencies and business operations.

10 fellows were selected from Haiti to work on real 
production-grade projects over 10 weeks. This is not 
a course. Every deliverable is something a real agency 
could deploy tomorrow.

Fellows get access to professional tools, real project 
briefs with acceptance criteria, a direct line into 
Gaya's network, and a Demo Day where the final product 
is presented to the Gaya team.

The program is operated on the ground by Ayiti AI, 
connecting Haitian engineering talent to US-based 
technology companies.

---

## About Gaya

Gaya builds AI automation systems for insurance 
agencies — helping teams eliminate manual work, 
accelerate follow-ups, and scale operations without 
adding headcount. Their stack includes workflow 
automation, voice AI agents, intelligent routing, 
and agentic task management.

## My Project Track

Throughout the 10 weeks, I am building two 
parallel systems that grow in complexity each module:

**Project 1 — n8n**
An automation workflow handling real agency 
operations. Starting with core plumbing in M1, 
adding error handling in M2, LLM intelligence 
in M3, an operator UI in M4, and production 
hardening through M5 and M6.

**Project 2 — Tasklet**
An agentic workflow system for agency operations 
management. Each module layers new capabilities — 
from basic task management in M1 to fully 
autonomous decision-making by Demo Day.
